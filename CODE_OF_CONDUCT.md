# Code of Conduct

Ember should be a place where careful technical disagreement is welcome and
people are treated with dignity.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful, direct, and specific. Critique the idea or change, not the
  person behind it.
- Make room for different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Support claims with evidence, and correct mistakes without theatre.
- Protect other people's privacy. Never publish personal photos, identity data,
  private paths, or private communications.
- Help keep issues and pull requests focused and useful.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
privacy violations, sustained personal attacks, spam, and repeated disruption
are not acceptable in project spaces or project-related private communication.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, issue a warning, restrict
participation temporarily, or ban someone permanently. Responses will be based
on the seriousness and pattern of the behavior, not status in the project.

Report conduct concerns through a private contact method listed on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/CarsonLMH). Do not put personal
details into a public issue. Reports will be reviewed as privately and fairly
as the circumstances allow; conflicts of interest should be disclosed.

This policy applies anywhere someone is representing Ember. It is original
project text informed by the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
